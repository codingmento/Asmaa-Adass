/ about

Design and business thinking, in one person.

Most developers build what they're told. I ask why first. Studying Business Administration alongside frontend development means I approach every project with the client's goals in mind, not just the interface — whether that's converting more visitors, looking credible to investors, or simply making a brand feel professional online.

I work across the full picture: structuring the UX in Figma, building it with clean HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, and making sure the result is fast, responsive, and easy to maintain long after I hand it off.
